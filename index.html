In progress

QualityIn progress

Launch quality checks on real homes

Every drawing (floor plans, elevations, site plan, roofs, ceilings) is checked against the 3D model. Automated checks are in place, and 24 of 38 faults from the latest drawings audit are fixed this week. Dimension strings, plan scales, elevation markers and a few extension options are still to do, and real-home checks on phone and tablet continue.

ComplianceIn progress

Irish planning rules engine

Rear, side, height, setback and boundary checks are in, plus overlooking, the Irish Water build-over distance and a site map on the report. Rules and report improvements are in, and real-home checks continue.

Planning searchIn progress

National planning portal coverage

Planning search now covers most councils across the country. Donegal, Monaghan and the former Cork City Council area are still to come.

AoifeIn progress

Aoife voice assistant

Talk to Aoife, an on-screen guide who uses the same design tools as the AI assistant. Voice, room picks, a furniture library showing all brands, a mute button, and memory of your earlier conversation are done. Final polish for phone and tablet is underway.
